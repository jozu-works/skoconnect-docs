## 3. Family Data CSV Template

### Instructions

1. Open the template below in a spreadsheet application (Excel, Google Sheets, etc.)
2. Enter **one row per parent/guardian** — if a student has two parents/guardians, create a separate row for each
3. **Do not** change the column headers
4. Save/export the file as **CSV (Comma Separated Values)** format
5. Email the completed file to: **info.skoconnect@agentmail.to**
6. **Deadline:** Within 3 business days of onboarding start

### Column Definitions

| Column | Description | Required | Example |
|--------|-------------|----------|---------|
| `student_first_name` | Student's first name | ✅ | Jada |
| `student_last_name` | Student's last name | ✅ | Thompson |
| `grade/class` | Grade level or class name | ✅ | Grade 4A |
| `parent_first_name` | Parent/guardian's first name | ✅ | Michelle |
| `parent_last_name` | Parent/guardian's last name | ✅ | Thompson |
| `parent_email` | Parent/guardian's email address | ✅ | michelle.t@email.com |
| `parent_phone` | Parent/guardian's phone number (876-XXX-XXXX) | ✅ | 876-555-1234 |
| `relationship` | Relationship to student | ✅ | Mother |

### Valid `relationship` Values

`Mother`, `Father`, `Guardian`, `Grandmother`, `Grandfather`, `Aunt`, `Uncle`, `Other`

### CSV Template

```csv
student_first_name,student_last_name,grade/class,parent_first_name,parent_last_name,parent_email,parent_phone,relationship
Jada,Thompson,Grade 4A,Michelle,Thompson,michelle.t@email.com,876-555-1234,Mother
Jada,Thompson,Grade 4A,David,Thompson,david.t@email.com,876-555-1235,Father
Jayden,Wilson,Grade 2B,Anika,Wilson,anika.w@email.com,876-555-2345,Mother
Aaliyah,Brown,Grade 5A,Karen,Brown,karen.b@email.com,876-555-3456,Mother
Aaliyah,Brown,Grade 5A,Michael,Brown,michael.b@email.com,876-555-3457,Father
```

> **⚠️ Data Privacy Notice:** All family data provided will be handled in strict compliance with the **Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA) 2020**. Data is used solely for setting up SkoConnect access at PGA Preparatory. SkoConnect will not share, sell, or repurpose this data. Full data processing agreement available upon request.

## 4. Training Agenda — Admin & Teacher Session

**Duration:** 1 Hour
**Format:** In-person or virtual (Zoom/Google Meet)
**Audience:** Principal, administrative staff, classroom teachers
**Led by:** Dre Newsome or SkoConnect Training Specialist
**Recommended timing:** Day 5 of onboarding

### Agenda

| Time | Topic | Details |
|------|-------|---------|
| **0:00 – 0:05** | **Welcome & Introductions** | Quick intros; confirm everyone can access the platform; set expectations for the session |
| **0:05 – 0:15** | **Why SkoConnect?** | Overview of the platform; why PGA is moving from WhatsApp to SkoConnect; benefits for teachers, admin, and parents; JDPA compliance overview |
| **0:15 – 0:25** | **Admin Dashboard Walkthrough** | Logging in; school profile overview; managing classes and student lists; viewing parent accounts; sending school-wide announcements |
| **0:25 – 0:40** | **Teacher Dashboard Walkthrough** | Logging in; class view; sending class-specific messages; sharing homework, events, and documents; managing attendance communications |
| **0:40 – 0:50** | **Hands-On Practice** | Each participant sends a test message to their class/group; admin sends a test school-wide notice; Q&A during practice |
| **0:50 – 0:55** | **Parent Experience Overview** | How parents will receive and respond to messages; parent login process; what the parent notification letter covers |
| **0:55 – 1:00** | **Next Steps & Support** | Go-live timeline; how to reach SkoConnect support (WhatsApp, phone, email); Day 14 check-in scheduling; closing remarks |

### Training Requirements

- Each participant should have a **laptop, desktop, or tablet** with internet access
- SkoConnect will provide **login credentials** before the session
- A **projector or shared screen** is helpful for in-person sessions
- Session will be **recorded** (with permission) for future reference

**RE: Important Update — New School Communication Platform**

Dear Parents and Guardians,

We are pleased to inform you that PGA Preparatory is transitioning to a **new school communication platform** called **SkoConnect**, effective [INSERT GO-LIVE DATE].

### Why Are We Making This Change?

Over the years, we have relied on **WhatsApp groups** to communicate with parents. While WhatsApp has served us well, as our school community has grown, we have encountered several challenges:

- **Missed messages** — Important announcements get lost in busy chat streams
- **No organized records** — It is difficult to track what was communicated and when
- **Privacy concerns** — WhatsApp groups expose parent phone numbers and do not meet modern data protection standards

**SkoConnect** is a dedicated school communication platform designed specifically for **Jamaican schools**. It provides a secure, organized, and reliable way for us to communicate with you about your child's education.

### What Does This Mean for You?

- **One app, everything in one place** — Announcements, homework, events, and messages from PGA Preparatory will all be accessible through SkoConnect
- **Class-specific and school-wide communication** — You will only receive information relevant to your child's class
- **No more scattered WhatsApp groups** — All school communication will be centralized
- **Free for parents** — There is no cost to you for using SkoConnect

### Your Data Privacy Matters

PGA Preparatory takes the protection of your personal information seriously. SkoConnect is fully compliant with the **Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA) 2020**. This means:

- Your personal information (phone number, email, name) is **securely stored** and **encrypted**
- Your data will **only** be used for school communication purposes
- Your information will **never** be shared with third parties or used for advertising
- You have the **right to access, correct, or request deletion** of your data at any time

WhatsApp does **not** offer JDPA-compliant data handling for schools. By moving to SkoConnect, we are ensuring that PGA Preparatory meets the highest standards of data privacy for our families.

### What Happens Next?

1. **You will receive login instructions** — SkoConnect will send you a welcome message with your login details and a simple guide to get started
2. **The transition will be gradual** — For the first two weeks, we will continue to post key announcements on both WhatsApp and SkoConnect
3. **Full transition** — After the two-week transition period, all school communication will move exclusively to SkoConnect

### Need Help?

If you have any questions or need assistance setting up SkoConnect, please do not hesitate to contact the school office at **876-988-2606** or email **pgaprep@gmail.com**. We are here to help.

We appreciate your partnership in making this transition smooth. This is an important step forward for PGA Preparatory, and we are confident it will strengthen the connection between our school and your family.

Warm regards,
