These emails target schools with demonstrated need for consolidated communication tools. The approach emphasizes local context, acknowledges Jamaican school communication challenges (WhatsApp fragmentation, paper forms, inconsistent emergency alerts), and offers a risk-free beta with no cost commitment. Tone is warm, professional, and action-oriented.
